# Scope turret lock

Parametric, 3D-printable lock that covers a riflescope's turrets so club
members cannot move the zero on club rifles. Fitted with the scope left in
its rings; one bolt, hex key, no padlock. Everything is generated by
`generate.py` (CadQuery) from `params.json` plus a scope profile. Same layout
as the Tikka monopod project (`../tikka monopo`).

## Working on it

- Scope profiles: `params.json` holds the shared lock / hardware settings;
  each scope has `scopes/<name>/scope.json` (its `scope` measurements, plus
  any `params.json` key it needs to override). New scope: copy
  `scopes/generic_30mm`, rename the folder, measure.
- Run: `venv\Scripts\python generate.py` builds every profile;
  `python generate.py scopes/<name>` builds one. About 30 s per profile with
  the clearance check on.
- Outputs (committed), in the profile's folder and named after it:
  `<name>_hood.stl`, `<name>_plate.stl` in print orientation, plus
  `<name>_preview.png`. Regenerate them with any geometry change.
- All lengths are in mm. Keys starting with `_` are notes. Unknown or
  missing keys stop the run.
- After a run, check:
  - no `ERROR:` lines (sanity checks in the generator);
  - `Checking clearances ... no overlaps` (hood coming straight down over
    the scope, plate sliding in from the left, fitted plate against scope
    and hood);
  - the Hardware line: "through the nut and the plate";
  - "Below the tube" against the room under the scope on the rifle;
  - the preview image.
- Coordinates: X along the tube (+X towards the eyepiece), Y across (+Y is
  the shooter's right, the windage side), Z up, X axis = tube centre line.
- Keep scratch scripts out of the project folder.
- `fit_test.py scopes/<name>`: 3 mm slices of the real hood (across through
  the turrets, along through the elevation turret), written to the profile's
  `fit_test/`, to check the scope measurements before the full print.
  Imports `generate.py` with the same argument.

## Design (current)

Hood from above + plate slid in underneath, like a pencil box lid. Chosen
(2026-10-20) so it fits without taking the scope out of its rings and so
that only a thin plate goes under the scope. It replaced a two-halves
clamshell split on the tube centre line (2 bolts), whose bottom half had to
come up from under the scope.

- **Hood:** cups over the elevation and windage turrets, a box round the
  turret housing, collars resting on the tube (legs straight down so they
  print), a lock block on the left. Below the tube centre line everything
  inside is straight down (no undercuts) so it drops on from above; the end
  walls and collars are slotted down where the tube passes.
- **Plate:** `PLATE_THICKNESS` flat plate, `PLATE_GAP` under the lowest part
  of the scope, slides in from the left through a slot in the lock block
  into grooves in the end walls, stops against the windage end wall. A
  finger tab sticks out of the block.
- **Lock:** one M5×25 upright through the lock block and the plate. Head and
  washer recessed in the top of the block; nut slid into a slot from the
  block's left face, above the plate, so the bolt is tightened against the
  block. The bolt end stays inside the floor under the plate. With the plate
  in, the hood cannot lift; with the bolt in, the plate cannot slide out.
- **Holding:** the turret housing stops the lock sliding along the tube and
  the turrets stop it turning. Nothing presses on the turrets. The hood can
  lift by about `PLATE_GAP` + clearances (~0.6 mm), so it may rattle a
  little.
- **Left knob:** parallax / illumination knob reachable through a slot; the
  bolt moves forward to clear it.
- **Hardware:** 1 × M5×25 socket head bolt, washer, plain hex nut. The user
  has M5×25 bolts and nuts; keep within `BOLT_LENGTH`.
- **Label:** "ZERO LOCKED" engraved on the lock block's left face.
- **Printing:** hood as fitted, open side down (bridges over the turret
  housing, in the cup ends and over the plate slot). Plate flat. The flat
  roof over the plate slot in the lock block is a 37 mm bridge (Nikko);
  support under it was very hard to remove. A 45° sloped roof was tried
  (2026-10-21) and rejected by the user: the flat roof gives a tighter
  fit and a stronger block. Keep it flat.
- **Bambu Studio (A1, PLA):** the user's project had the hoods turned onto
  their side with supports on: 11 h 16 m for 6 sets. Upright, supports
  off, 0.28 mm: 5 h 34 m (`<name>_x6_upright_0.28.3mf`). At 0.20 / 0.24 mm
  the slicer warns "floating cantilever" on the hood (source not found;
  not the plate slot).

## Profiles

- `generic_30mm`: guessed typical 30 mm scope with a side parallax knob.
  Template only. 70 mm long, 5.8 mm below the tube.
- `nikko_mountmaster_3-9x40ao`: the club scope, Nikko Stirling Mountmaster
  3-9x40 AO (NMM3940AO). Published: 1 inch tube, 322 mm long, capped
  turrets, parallax on the objective bell (no left knob); no retailer
  publishes housing or turret sizes (Optics Warehouse blocks automated
  fetches). Housing length 32, height 28 and drop 1.5 (it hangs below the
  tube) measured from the user's side photo scaled off the tube (2026-10-20,
  about ±1 mm). Housing width and turret caps (photo had the cap off) are
  still estimates. 50 mm long, 5.3 mm below the tube.

## Open questions / things to check on the first print

- The scope is modelled as a plain box (turret housing) with plain cylinders
  (turrets), turrets centred on the housing. Scopes with turrets off-centre
  on the housing need the model extended.
- Room under the scope: the hood goes 5.3 mm below the Nikko tube (plate
  3.9). If the rifle has less, thin `PLATE_THICKNESS`, `GROOVE_LIP` or
  `PLATE_GAP`.
- The lock block sticks out 17.6 mm left of the Nikko tube and goes down to
  the hood bottom; check it clears the stock / receiver beside the scope.
- Bridges over the turret housing and plate slot may sag; CLEARANCE (0.8)
  allows for some.
- Anyone with a 4 mm hex key can take it off. If that turns out not to be
  enough, a tamper-proof bolt (pin hex / pin Torx) fits the same hole.
